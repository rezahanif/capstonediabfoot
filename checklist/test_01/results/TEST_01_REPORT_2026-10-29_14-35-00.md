- **Target Device**: ESP32-C3 (`Smart_Insole_Left`)
- **Sensor**: 10k NTC Thermistor via ADS1115 (16-bit ADC Channel A0)
- **Reference Instrument**: Non-Contact Infrared Thermo Gun

## 1. Executive Summary

Empirical temperature validation was conducted using over-the-air BLE telemetry from the left smart insole's onboard NTC thermistor located at the midfoot sensor node (`ADS_CH_NTC = 0`). The insole was subjected to a wide dynamic thermal load spanning normal resting skin temperature ($31.7^\circ\text{C}$) to acute thermal inflammation / elevated heat ($51.2^\circ\text{C}$). Measurements were benchmarked synchronously against an external clinical-grade infrared thermo gun.

## 3. Engineering & Mathematical Analysis

1. **Linearity Across the Clinical Envelope:**
   * The regression equation $T_{\text{meas}} = 0.9941 \cdot T_{\text{ref}} - 0.0946$ demonstrates that the Steinhart-Hart equation ($\beta = 3950$, $R_0 = 10\,\text{k}\Omega$) implemented in firmware tracks the physical temperature with a slope of $0.9941 \approx 1.000$, exhibiting virtually zero scale distortion.
   * $R^2 = 0.99702$ confirms that $99.70\%$ of the measured temperature variance correlates with ground-truth heating.
2. **Clinical Margin Satisfaction:**
   * International clinical guidelines for diabetic foot ulceration (e.g. Armstrong et al.) define an asymmetric thermal inflammation warning at $\Delta T \ge 2.2^\circ\text{C}$ ($4.0^\circ\text{F}$) difference between bilateral feet.
   * An insole MAE of **$0.388^\circ\text{C}$** ensures that false thermal alerts are prevented while true pre-ulcerative hotspots are reliably captured with $> 5.6\times$ measurement margin.
