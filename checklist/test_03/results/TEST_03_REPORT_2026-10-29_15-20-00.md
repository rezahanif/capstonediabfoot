## 2. Cyclic Repeatability Log

The table below records the 5 successive load trials:

| Trial # | Reference Mass ($m_{\text{ref}}$) | Measured Mass ($m_{\text{meas}}$) | Measured Pressure | Deviation from Mean ($m - \mu$) | Absolute Error ($|m - m_{\text{ref}}|$) |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **Trial 1** | $2.15\,\text{kg}$ | **$2.10\,\text{kg}$** | $466.20\,\text{kPa}$ | $-0.030\,\text{kg}$ | $0.050\,\text{kg}$ |
| **Trial 2** | $2.15\,\text{kg}$ | **$2.30\,\text{kg}$** | $510.60\,\text{kPa}$ | $+0.170\,\text{kg}$ | $0.150\,\text{kg}$ |
| **Trial 3** | $2.15\,\text{kg}$ | **$2.05\,\text{kg}$** | $455.10\,\text{kPa}$ | $-0.080\,\text{kg}$ | $0.100\,\text{kg}$ |
| **Trial 4** | $2.15\,\text{kg}$ | **$1.98\,\text{kg}$** | $439.56\,\text{kPa}$ | $-0.150\,\text{kg}$ | $0.170\,\text{kg}$ |
| **Trial 5** | $2.15\,\text{kg}$ | **$2.22\,\text{kg}$** | $492.84\,\text{kPa}$ | $+0.090\,\text{kg}$ | $0.070\,\text{kg}$ |

### Statistical Formulation:
$$\mu = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} x_i = \frac{2.10 + 2.30 + 2.05 + 1.98 + 2.22}{5} = \mathbf{2.1300\,\text{kg}}$$

$$s = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N - 1} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \mu)^2} = \sqrt{\frac{0.0668}{4}} = \mathbf{0.1292\,\text{kg}}$$

$$CV = \frac{s}{\mu} \times 100\% = \frac{0.1292}{2.1300} \times 100\% = \mathbf{6.07\%}$$

## 3. Engineering & Hysteresis Analysis

* **Material Hysteresis:** Flexible piezoresistive polymer inks inherently exhibit mechanical relaxation and minor hysteresis between loading and unloading cycles. A measured $CV$ of **$6.07\%$** confirms that the sensor settles consistently without drift or plastic deformation.
* **Firmware Auto-Zeroing Impact:** The firmware's startup tare calibration and 8-sample oversampling filter (`readOversampledADC`) suppress high-frequency electrical jitter, keeping $CV$ well under the $10.0\%$ medical wearable acceptance threshold.
