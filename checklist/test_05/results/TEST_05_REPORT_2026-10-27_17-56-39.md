### 4. Technical Findings & Root Cause Analysis

1. **SWING Exact Match:** The measured swing duration ($36.0\%$) aligns with standard human clinical literature ($35 - 40\%$).
2. **Coupled Midstance / Toe-Off Offset:** 
   * In Steps 1 to 12 (where heel contact was confirmed on sensor S5), Midstance registered **$21.9\%$**, falling inside the normal range ($20 - 35\%$).
   * In Steps 13 to 20, the heel missed the conductive sensor disc ($0.0\,\text{kPa}$), transferring body weight entirely onto the forefoot ($577 - 864\,\text{kPa}$). This shifted full-foot Midstance frames into forefoot Toe-Off frames ($58.3\%$ in those steps), causing Midstance to finish $5.1\%$ below range and Toe-Off $8.6\%$ above range.
3. **Hardware Pin Correction:** The ESP32-C3 firmware has `PIN_HEEL` mapped to GPIO 0 and `PIN_MTH4` mapped to GPIO 4. On the physical flex PCB, GPIO 4 is the Heel sensor. Both test scripts and the app normalize this via $P_{\text{heel\_eff}} = \max(P_{\text{heel}}, P_{\text{mth4}})$.
