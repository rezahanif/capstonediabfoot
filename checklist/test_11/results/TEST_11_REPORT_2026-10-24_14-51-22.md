## 2. Engineering Roadmap to 8.0+ Hours

1. **Hardware Option (Standard Wearable Form Factor):**
   - Insole arch cavity fits a standard 800–1000 mAh ultra-slim pouch LiPo (e.g. 503048 format, 5mm thickness).
   - At 101.5 mA draw, an 850 mAh cell yields **8.37 hours** of continuous streaming.
2. **Firmware Option (Gait Cadence & BLE Duty Cycle Optimization):**
   - Current draw is dominated by the ESP32-C3 2.4 GHz RF synthesizer being awake.
   - Using BLE connection interval optimization, batching packets, or on-chip step event transmission drops average draw from 101 mA to $< 30\,\text{mA}$, giving $> 10\,\text{hours}$ on 300 mAh.
